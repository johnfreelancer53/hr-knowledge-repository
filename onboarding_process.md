# Employee Onboarding Process

## 🎯 Objective

To ensure smooth integration of new employees into the organization.

---

## 📅 Pre-Joining

* Offer acceptance
* Documentation collection
* IT setup

---

## 📅 Day 1

* Welcome session
* Company overview
* Policy briefing

---

## 📅 Week 1

* Department introduction
* Role clarity
* Assign mentor

---

## 📅 Month 1

* Initial performance feedback
* Training sessions

---

## 📅 3-Month Review

* Probation evaluation
* Performance assessment
* Confirmation decision

---

## 📊 KPI Metrics

* New hire retention rate
* Time to productivity
* Employee satisfaction
