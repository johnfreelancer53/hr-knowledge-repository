# Leave Policy

## 📌 Annual Leave

* 30 calendar days per year after 1 year of service

## 📌 Sick Leave

* 90 days per year:

  * First 15 days: Full pay
  * Next 30 days: Half pay
  * Remaining: Unpaid

## 📌 Maternity Leave

* 60 days:

  * 45 days full pay
  * 15 days half pay

## 📌 Public Holidays

* As declared by UAE government

---

## 🎯 Purpose

To ensure fair and compliant leave practices aligned with UAE labour law.
