# Recruitment Workflow

## 🎯 Objective

To establish a structured and efficient hiring process that ensures quality talent acquisition.

---

## 📌 Step 1: Manpower Requisition

* Hiring manager submits request
* Includes:

  * Job title
  * Justification
  * Budget approval
  * Reporting structure

---

## 📌 Step 2: Job Description Creation

* Define:

  * Key responsibilities
  * Required qualifications
  * Experience level
  * Competencies

---

## 📌 Step 3: Sourcing Candidates

* Job portals
* LinkedIn sourcing
* Recruitment agencies
* Internal referrals

---

## 📌 Step 4: Screening

* Resume shortlisting
* Initial HR screening call
* Salary expectations alignment

---

## 📌 Step 5: Interview Process

* Technical interview
* Behavioral interview
* Final management round

---

## 📌 Step 6: Offer Management

* Prepare offer letter
* Salary benchmarking
* Approval from leadership

---

## 📌 Step 7: Pre-Onboarding

* Document collection
* Background verification
* System setup

---

## 📌 Step 8: Onboarding

* Orientation session
* Policy briefing
* Team introduction

---

## 📊 KPI Metrics

* Time-to-hire
* Cost-per-hire
* Offer acceptance rate
